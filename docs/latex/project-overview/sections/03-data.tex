\section{Critical Integrity Rules}
\begin{itemize}
\item Normalized email is unique. Passwords and refresh tokens are never stored in plaintext. Roles are CUSTOMER, STAFF and ADMIN.
\item Prices, totals and payment amounts are integer VND. Available stock is nonnegative and purchase quantities are positive integers. Binary floating point is not used for money.
\item PostgreSQL partial unique indexes or equivalent constraints enforce at most one PENDING and one SUCCESS attempt per order.
\item Order items and shipping details are purchase snapshots. Later profile or catalog changes do not rewrite purchase history.
\item Role changes and their audits, stock adjustments and their audits, checkout and reservation, settlement and history each commit or roll back together.
\item Unverified callback data is not accepted payment evidence. Reconciliation logs omit secrets and unnecessarily sensitive raw payloads.
\end{itemize}

Generated line totals, partial unique indexes and transactional business rules are essential to the physical design.

\section{Inventory and Image Storage}
Checkout deducts available stock as a reservation in the order transaction. Successful IPN settlement does not deduct it again. Only eligible pre-payment cancellation or order expiration releases it; a conditional state transition determines which transaction may release stock. Attempt expiration alone leaves inventory unchanged.

Initial stock is accepted only during product creation. Subsequent Admin changes require a reasoned quantity delta and audit, rather than a general catalog update.

Images reside in a configured application directory mounted through a named volume. PostgreSQL stores relative paths. If file storage succeeds but its database update fails, the application attempts orphan-file deletion and safely logs a cleanup failure.
